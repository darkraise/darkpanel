## Delta

**UI/UX depth:** touch — update transaction states get their UI-4 badge variants

- **UI-1** — `pending`, `downloading`, `staged`, `swapped` and `rolling-back` history badges render `secondary`.
- **UI-2** — A `rolled-back` history badge renders `destructive`; its error shows in the Error column at 1440x900 (at 768x1024 the Error column lies beyond the table's own scroll edge and at 375x812 it is truncated, so the screenshots there show the badge only).
- **UI-3** — `committed` stays `default` and `failed` stays `destructive`.
- **UI-4** — Every history badge shows its state as text.
- **UI-5** — Rollout and rollout-host badges are unchanged: the Rollouts page still shows `succeeded` and `committed` as `default`, `running`, `updating` and `waiting` as `secondary`, `failed` as `destructive`, `skipped` and `cancelled` as `outline`.
- **OP-1** — Proven at 375x812, 768x1024 and 1440x900, light and dark, with no axe violation on the host page showing all eight transaction states.
